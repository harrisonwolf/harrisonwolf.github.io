01Projects

Algebra search engine

70–115× faster than Macaulay2
C++ · hand-written core · repo · arXiv:2512.24320

Testing a proposed rule in algebraic geometry meant sifting tens of billions of structures for the rare ones that break it, more than Macaulay2, the standard tool, could reach. I hand-wrote a ~1,200-line C++ engine in 2024 that does it, 70–115× faster on matched searches and validated case by case; its exhaustive search surfaced the counterexamples behind one of the preprint’s three main theorems.

Runtime vs. candidate-sequence count, codimensions 3–7, against Macaulay2 on the same machine (linear axes).

Neighborhood-intelligence engine

six-source pipeline with a separate serving runtime
Python · core in ~3 months · private product repo · public Audit Edition

Choosing where to live means weighing schools, safety and prices from six public sources that never agreed on a format, and a ranking is only worth trusting if every number traces to its source. Nexora ranks Fresno County neighborhoods against a family’s budget and priorities on real county GIS parcels, with every view stamped with its data lineage. I designed and built the whole system from scratch, from the data architecture to the map interface, orchestrating coding agents for most of the implementation.

The surface: the agent workspace on Fresno County. The footer stamps every view with its data lineage.

Video generation on an 8 GB GPU

two open-weight models, one 8 GB card
Python · core in ~2 weeks · repo

Open-weight video models want far more GPU memory than most people have, and chaining short clips into longer scenes invites seams and drift. This studio runs two of them on one 8 GB GPU from a terminal UI, with seam and drift telemetry and a blind A/B harness so changes can be measured, not eyeballed. I built the orchestration, UI and measurement layer; the models were trained by others.

Geometry engine from first principles

The geometry behind games and maps
C++ · hand-written foundation · later agent-assisted · standard library only · repo

Meshes and Voronoi regions underpin games, maps and simulations, and they fail in subtle ways when rounding misjudges which side of a line a point lies on. This engine triangulates polygons and builds Delaunay meshes and Voronoi regions using only the C++ standard library, with scale-aware predicates pinned down by 1,027 test assertions. I hand-wrote the primitives, polygon operations and original ear clipper, and directed agents on the Delaunay/Voronoi layers and harness.

One random 15-vertex polygon (left) cut into 13 triangles by the ear-clipper (right) — n−2, as a simple polygon must give.

02Terrain demo

Terrain studies

Geometry engine + GIS platform · portfolio demonstration · full case study

Before anyone buys or designs a site they need numbers they can’t eyeball: how much earth must move, where the rain goes, how much a basin could hold. These three studies compute all three from public USGS elevation alone, on my geometry engine and the Nexora platform. Preliminary studies, suitable for scoping a site; detailed design still needs a licensed engineer.

  • A level pad on a sloped lot: 9,160 cubic yards moved, cut balancing fill.
  • A river bluff’s rainfall routed: 604k gallons from a one-inch storm, and where it leaves the property.
  • A dry canyon dammed at its one outlet: 44,457 acre-feet behind a 256-foot dam.
Before any engineering: a building lot, a river bluff, and a dry canyon a dam could close.
